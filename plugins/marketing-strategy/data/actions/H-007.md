# H-007 — Homepage hero: "Clone the template" CTA above the fold

## Action

1. Open `/var/www/html/systemprompt-web/services/web/config.yaml` and find the homepage hero content (or wherever the homepage template lives — likely `extensions/web/src/homepage/` or `services/content/pages/`).
2. Replace the current primary CTA text with something similar to: **"Clone the template — own the binary in 10 minutes"**. Link target: `https://github.com/systempromptio/systemprompt-template`.
